Profile

A public version of who I am professionally and creatively, without putting my private life on display.

I have spent years around global tech platforms, digital tools, language systems, and the messy space where people and software meet.

Clear thinking matters. So does taste, judgment, and knowing what not to overexplain.

My work has moved across technology companies, education platforms, localization, training material, documentation, user operations, and internal systems.

The common thread is simple: take something complicated, understand how it works, then make it easier to use, explain, improve, or scale.

Outside of work, I’m interested in improv, storytelling, software, AI, machine learning, football, travel, and the fictional universes that shaped a lot of modern imagination.
